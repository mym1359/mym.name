How the review works

Affordability. The review subtracts essentials and a stated reserve from take-home income. Plans add arrears and any modeled fee to the regular payment.

Timing. A due-date move must bridge a timing mismatch and the next payment must cover both the regular amount and the arrears. A budget fit can still require a check of day-of-payment cash.

Evidence. Scenarios are hand-written. Tests check rule behavior. Real-world impact, policy eligibility, changes to contractual interest and total loan cost are outside this analysis.
